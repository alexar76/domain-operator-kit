# Provenance

Domain Operator Kit lives at `domain-kit/` in the AIMarket monorepo and publishes as the satellite [alexar76/domain-operator-kit](https://github.com/alexar76/domain-operator-kit). It has no runtime dependency on a parent checkout.

`src/payments/reconcile.js` and the six tests in `test/reconcile.test.js` were adapted from the corresponding files in [eSIM Operator Kit](https://github.com/alexar76/esim-operator-kit), originally maintained in `aicom/esim-kit/rails/`. The original MIT copyright notice is preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The hero and badge SVGs in `assets/` are repository-local artwork. They do not imply endorsement by Openprovider, Vercel, Coinbase, OpenAI or a third-party agent client. The local-test badge is a dated verification snapshot, not a live CI indicator.
